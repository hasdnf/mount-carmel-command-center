# Mount Carmel news source notes

## Pennsylvania Governor newsroom — Executive Order 2026-05
URL: https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2026-press-releases/governor-shapiro-signs-executive-order-on-data-center-developmen

Read on 2026-11-08. The order directs DEP to require GRID commitments for data-center proposals, requires local approvals before permits issue, removes AI data-center projects from the PA Permit Fast Track Program, and emphasizes energy affordability, community engagement, workforce/economic development, transparency, and environmental protection. It also states that data centers must pay the full cost of new energy infrastructure needed to power the project and that public transparency requirements apply.

## K&L Gates analysis
